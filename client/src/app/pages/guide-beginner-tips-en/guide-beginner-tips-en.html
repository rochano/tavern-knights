Tavern Knights tips for beginners

Cleared the stage but missing stars, or chasing the weekly ranking? These tips come straight from the game rules.

▶ Play free

Getting all three stars

★Clear the stage
★★Clear it in under 5 minutes
★★★No knight gets knocked out during the stage

The third star is the hard one. Swap out a knight as soon as their health runs low, and bring Old Bart for a healing beer in a tight spot.

The secret room behind the cracked wall

Each stage has a cracked wall only Chef Gorn can smash, so pick him on the world map first. In stage 1 the room holds a chest with the Truffle of Ages and two extra Glowcaps.

You can bring two crew members. Chef Gorn plus Old Bart is the best beginner pair: the secret room and a heal.

King Shroomba

  • Arrive with a full gauge and open with the ultimate.
  • Use Rosemary from range while he sprays spores, then swap to Arthur when he leaves an opening.
  • Hidden drop: defeat him while he is burning to get the Ember Spore instead of the Shroom Crown. Land Arthur's fire wave before the final hit.

Stage 2, the Ancient Wine Dungeon, has a hidden boss drop too: try finishing the boss while Percival's ice has it frozen.

Swap with purpose

  • Big groups: Arthur's fire wave pierces the whole line.
  • High or distant enemies: Rosemary's stars (on a computer, click to aim).
  • Fast or tough enemies: slow or freeze them with Percival, then swap in to hit.

Climbing the weekly ranking

Points come from monsters (100 each), mimics (500), pickups (20 to 50) and the boss (5000). Mimics pose as treasure chests but are worth a lot.

There are separate boards for stage score and stage time: rush for time, or clear every monster and mimic for score. Weeks reset on Monday, Thai time.

Where to spend gold

After each shift, take your gold and ingredients to the alchemy and forge room. Upgrade weapons first to clear stages faster, then buy tavern tools such as the spoon, which makes customers pay more.

FAQ

How do I get 3 stars in Tavern Knights?

Clear the stage in under 5 minutes without any knight getting knocked out.

Where is the secret room in Tavern Knights?

Behind a cracked wall in the stage. Pick Chef Gorn as crew on the world map, then press C to have him smash it.

How do I get King Shroomba's hidden drop?

Defeat him while he is burning to get the Ember Spore. Land Arthur's fire wave before the final hit.
