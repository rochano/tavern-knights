Tavern night shift guide: 5 stars

The night shift lasts 80 seconds and its gold pays for your upgrades. Here is how stars and pay are worked out, and what to do when.

▶ Play free

How stars are scored

Stars come from customers served minus angry customers who walked out (1.5 each), divided by 4, up to five stars.

For five stars, serve around 20 customers with nobody walking out. One walk-out costs almost two servings.

Give each job to the right knight

  • Rosemary runs the kitchen. The more notes you hit, the better the dish and the higher the price.
  • Percival pours and serves. Stop the bar in the gold band for a "Perfect pour!"; stop early and it is all foam.
  • Arthur washes and clears plates and bonks drunks. You start with four clean bowls; when they run out the kitchen stops.

Earning more gold

  • Dragon King Tom Yum uses a Glowcap from the stages and sells for far more than House Fried Rice.
  • VIPs pay double but have less patience. Have food ready before they arrive.
  • Serve fast: customers who still have more than half their patience pay 25% extra.
  • The spoon from the forge makes every customer pay 20% more.

Shift events, in order

Watch the alert bar at the bottom: the game warns you before each event. When customers get impatient, Percival's harp calms them down.

Pre-shift checklist

  1. Collect as many Glowcaps as you can in the stages.
  2. At the start, have Rosemary cook two dishes ahead.
  3. Keep Arthur washing whenever he is free.
  4. Mid-shift, get ready for the thief and the fire.
  5. Your takings go on the weekly ranking automatically.

FAQ

How do I get 5 stars in the Tavern Knights night shift?

Serve about 20 customers with nobody walking out. Stars are served minus 1.5 per walk-out, divided by 4.

How do I earn more gold in the tavern?

Cook Dragon King Tom Yum with Glowcaps, serve VIPs (they pay double), serve fast for a 25% bonus and buy the spoon at the forge.

What do I do when the kitchen catches fire?

Swap to Rosemary, go to the kitchen and mash the Skill button until the fire is out.
