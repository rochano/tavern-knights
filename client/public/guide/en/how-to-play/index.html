How to play Tavern Knights

The game switches between two halves: fight through stages to gather ingredients, then run the tavern to earn gold. Here is each step, with keyboard and touch controls.

▶ Play free

One round of play

  1. World map: pick a stage and up to two crew members.
  2. Stage: defeat monsters, collect ingredients, find the secret room and beat the boss.
  3. Results: time, score, stars and your weekly rank.
  4. Night shift at The Tipsy Dragon: cook, pour beer, serve customers and earn gold.
  5. Alchemy and forge: spend ingredients and gold on weapons, armour, food and tools, then head out again.

Progress is saved in your own browser. No account needed.

On a phone, turn it sideways. Push the left stick to move and up to jump; the sword button attacks and the smaller buttons are special, ultimate, swap and crew.

The three knights

  • Arthur: close-range fire sword. His special, a fire wave, pierces a row of enemies and sets them burning.
  • Rosemary: ranged stars, safe against high or distant enemies. Her special drops a giant bowl of tom yum.
  • Percival: ice bolts that slow and freeze. His special is a meteor shower.

Landing hits fills the gauge. Half a gauge buys a special; a full gauge unleashes the Ultimate Tavern Combo, which sweeps the whole screen.

Crew

  • Chef Gorn smashes cracked walls, opening the way to secret rooms.
  • Old Bart sends a healing beer.

Choose your crew on the world map, then press C in a stage to call them.

In the tavern

KitchenRosemary cooks: press the arrows as the notes reach the circle.
Beer tapPercival pours: press again to stop in the gold band.
TablesPercival serves; Arthur clears plates and bonks drunks.
SinkArthur washes dishes. No clean bowls means no cooking.

Click a station and the game switches to the right worker for you; on a phone just tap it. Full strategy in the night shift guide.

FAQ

Can I play Tavern Knights on my phone?

Yes. Turn the phone sideways and use the on-screen stick and buttons, though keyboard and mouse on a computer is easiest.

Do I need to download Tavern Knights?

No. Open tavern-knights.pages.dev in a browser and play, free and with no sign-up.

How do I switch to English?

Options > Language in the game, or open the link with ?lang=en at the end.
