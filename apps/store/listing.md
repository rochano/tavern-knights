## English (en)

**App name (30):** Tavern Knights

**iOS subtitle (30):** 16-bit Action & Tavern Shifts

**Google Play short description (80):** Three broke knights: slay monsters by day, run a rowdy tavern by night!

**iOS promotional text (170):** A dishwasher knight, a cat-eared chef and an ice-harp bard storm the Poison Mushroom Forest, topple King Shroomba, then pull the night shift at The Tipsy Dragon.

**Full description (4000):**

Tavern Knights is a 90s-style 16-bit side-scrolling action game with a tavern-management twist.

By day, three down-on-their-luck knights fight through dungeons:
• Arthur the Dishwasher: flaming sword and skillet shield, an all-rounder
• Rosemary the Chef: cat-eared brawler with spinning twin claws
• Percival the Bard: rains ice arrows from range and slows foes with his harp
Swap knights at any time, unleash specials, and when the gauge is full, call down the Ultimate Tavern Combo: a giant boiling pot of tom yum soup that clears the screen.

By night, run The Tipsy Dragon tavern:
• Wash dishes, bounce rowdy drunks, cook in a rhythm mini-game and pour the perfect jelly beer
• Handle VIP guests, robbers and kitchen fires

Bring rare ingredients home from bosses, brew potions and forge gear in the secret cellar. Upgrade to Iron, Dragon, Royal Gold and the legendary Master Chef Set, each with its own look.

• Hand-made pixel art and an original soundtrack
• Touch controls, virtual joystick, keyboard and mouse
• English and Thai
• Plays offline, no ads

Made by Rochano Developer Teams
